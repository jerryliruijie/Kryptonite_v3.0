# Development representation comparison

These saved results compare raw and sign-transformed inputs for five classifiers
on three training-fold splits. They are retained because the current paper says
the usefulness of sign inputs depends on the classifier. They are development
evidence, not local-test results and not Figure 2 of the current paper.

The files were moved without changing their bytes from the old `paper/data/`
copies. Their SHA-256 hashes are:
